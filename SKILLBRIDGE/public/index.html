How a swap actually works

No points, no credits, no invoices. Just two people and a shared calendar.

01

Post what you can teach and what you're after

Onyekachi listed upright bass and asked for Yoruba conversation practice. That's the whole listing — no pricing tiers to configure.

02

SkillBridge finds the other half of the trade

Matches surface when what you offer lines up with what someone else needs, and what you need lines up with what they offer.

03

Agree on hours, then meet

Most members trade hour-for-hour. Desmond built a dry-stone wall over one Saturday in exchange for six weeks of French drills.
