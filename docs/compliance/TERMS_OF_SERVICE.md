## 2. GHANA CARD IDENTITY OBOARDING & KYC MANDATE
In compliance with the Data Protection Act of Ghana (Act 843), all micro-vendors, elite wholesalers, and independent logistics riders must submit a verified national identity token (Ghana Card PIN) before accessing any transactional wallets. 
* All uploaded credentials are encrypted and mapped directly to user data rows.
* Profiles using false names or non-matching carrier identity tags will face immediate suspension.

## 3. ATOMIC FINANCIAL TRANSACTIONS & COMMISSIONS SPLITS
By listing products as an Elite Wholesaler or Micro Vendor, you authorize the SkillBridge core database engine to process automated transaction splits upon checkout clearance:
* **Wholesaler Split Rate:** A dynamic flat fee or 10% platform marketplace commission cut is deducted from gross order volumes automatically.
* **Micro Vendor Fee:** A default processing fee of 5% applies to all standard retail vendor cash transactions.
* Deducted cuts land instantly inside the Admin Revenue Vault ledger, while remainder shares are dropped directly into the seller's active vault balance sheet row.

## 4. MOBILE MONEY DISBURSEMENT BARRIERS & SAFEGUARDS
Vendors and users may disburse earnings into their linked mobile wallets at any time subject to system safety bounds:
* **Minimum Threshold:** Outbound cashouts must meet or exceed a strict minimum protection barrier of 50.00 GHS.
* **Network Carriers:** Supported routing codes include MTN Mobile Money (MTN), Telecel Cash (VOD), and AirtelTigo Money (ATL).
* All transactions are securely routed using automated cryptographic hash checks on Paystack's API.

## 5. GEOSPATIAL LOCATION DATA TRACING
Independent delivery riders must allow continuous real-time GPS telemetry coordinate data logs (latitude, longitude, speed, and bearing metrics) while marked as available. This tracking ensures delivery time tracking, customer maps visibility, and automated milestone settlement records.
